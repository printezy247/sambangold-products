## Problem

- Challenge fees run $500–$5,000 and roughly nine in ten attempts fail.
- The rules that actually fail people are buried in the terms, not on the sales page.

## Solution

Expected value on the fee, plus a red/yellow scan of pasted terms for the rules that actually fail people.

## Commands

| Command | Does |
|:--|:--|
| `/propcalc FEE SIZE PASS%` | expected value for one challenge |
